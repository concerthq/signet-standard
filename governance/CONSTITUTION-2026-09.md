# Constitution of the Standards Committee

**Status:** Draft instrument — takes effect when executed at the first Committee session. Until executed it decides nothing.
**Tier on execution:** the appointments and the rules in §3–§5 are governance acts of Tier 2 weight (they change who reviews and how changes adopt); the instrument itself follows the GOVERNANCE.md rule that changes to the review rules carry a comment period. The comment period runs pre-session so execution is not delayed by it.
**Names:** withheld here by the standing rule (D-19). Members are identified in this instrument by role register entry; the register names them at execution, by its own append-only route.

## 1. Recital

`GOVERNANCE.md` provided that the Committee "will be constituted at the first external certification" — an externally observable trigger chosen so constitution could not be quietly deferred. Constitution now proceeds **earlier** than that trigger, by appointment, because two qualified candidates are available and the interim arrangement's central limitation — both repository identities operated by one natural person, review that is procedural rather than independent — is better ended early than preserved for a trigger that guaranteed only a latest date. The trigger was a floor against deferral, not a bar against readiness. This instrument records the change of trigger as a decision; `GOVERNANCE.md` is amended accordingly (edits attached) so the two records do not disagree.

## 2. Appointments

Two members are appointed, effective on execution, each on the basis of a completed assessment under `governance/role-competency-framework.md` recorded per `governance/person-assessment.md` **before** execution. Appointment without a completed assessment is void under this instrument. Entries append to `governance/role-register.md` at execution. The steward attends, briefs, and drafts; the steward does not vote (§3).

## 3. Decision rule

1. **Quorum** is both members.
2. **Adoption is by unanimity** of the members. The steward is non-voting.
3. **Deadlock is a result, not a failure:** a proposal that does not achieve unanimity remains Draft, with the disagreement recorded in the proposal (a gate, named), and may return only with new argument — the same discipline `declined alternatives` already applies.
4. **Recusal:** a recused matter is decided by the remaining member alone; if that member is also recused, the matter is deferred and recorded. Recusals are declared against the interests register (§5) before discussion, not after.
5. This rule is stated now, in advance of any disagreement, because a deadlock rule invented during the first disagreement is not a rule.

## 4. First-session business

The Committee at its first session receives, in this order (agenda attached as `first-session-agenda.md`):

1. **Ratification review of the twenty interim resolutions in force**, indexed in `governance/README.md`. A proposed disposition list is attached to the agenda: bulk-ratify the mechanism resolutions; individually review those that decide vocabulary or scope. `IR-2026-08` is treated as its own record recommends — one resolution acknowledging the eleven prior normative changes, not eleven approvals.
2. **The interests and recusals register initiates** (§5) with its first two entries.
3. **IAR-0006 expires** on execution; proposals registered under it stand registered; any completed comment periods are noted.
4. **SE-0001** is received with the recusal attached.
5. The four **shape gates** (ET-2, PA-2, MS-3, C-1) are scheduled, not decided, unless the members elect otherwise.

## 5. Interests and recusals register

Initiates at execution as `governance/interests-register.md` (draft attached), append-only, indexed from `governance/README.md`. Entry 1: the steward's dual role (steward; advisor to a deploying implementer) and dual-account operation to date. Entry 2: the recusal on SE-0001 and `CP-Requirement-Sockets`, requested pre-constitution and satisfiable from execution. Member interests are declared at execution before any adoption vote.

## 6. Mechanical handover (completes within 7 days of execution)

Review passes to accounts under separate control: member accounts gain review rights; `CODEOWNERS` is updated so normative paths (including `state-model/state-model.json` — closing the D-31 class) require a member review; branch protection reflects it; `concertcustodian` ceases to be a sufficient approver for Tier 2. `GOVERNANCE.md` "Repository identities" is amended to describe the new arrangement (edits attached). Until the handover completes, no Tier 2 change merges.

## 7. What this instrument does not do

It adopts no proposal, closes no defect, and ratifies nothing by implication. Ratification of each interim resolution is an act of the Committee on the record.
