## Rules

1. A declaration precedes discussion of any matter it touches.
2. An entry states the interest and the standing consequence; it does not adjudicate merit.
3. Absence of an entry is a claim — the register is the single record, so "no interest declared" is checkable, and a late declaration appends with its lateness stated.
