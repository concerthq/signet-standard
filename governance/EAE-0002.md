# EAE-0002 – Implementer enquiry: auction extension readiness

**Received:** 14 September 2026, against v0.17.1. A written readiness review of the auction
extension and the `auction-platform` profile, retained off-tree by the steward.
**Originator (role-based):** an engineer at the deploying implementer in which the steward holds
a declared interest, preparing a headless reverse-auction service and demonstration front end
(`governance/interests-register.md` entries 1 and 5).
**Status:** Decomposed. Each row lands by its own route; this record does not decide anything.
**Location note:** a Concert-authored record, at `governance/` inside the naming deny-list's
coverage, like EAE-0001. The originating organisation is not named in any Foundation artifact.

## Interest consequence (recorded at intake)

The steward registers proposals originating from this implementer but does not adopt them. No
interim resolution under the bootstrap clause is used for `CP-Auction-Determinism` or for any
proposal derived from this enquiry; adoption is reserved to the Standards Committee. Once the
Committee is constituted, the steward recuses from voting on any proposal originating from this
implementer (interests register entry 5; IAR-0006 §4; constitution §3.4).

This enquiry originates inside the steward's declared conflict perimeter, and the steward's
existing machinery covers it: the steward is non-voting, and interests register entries 1, 2 and 5
apply. No Committee member recusal arises from this enquiry, and the member recusals recorded for
EAE-0001 do not extend to it.

Until the Committee adopts, certification tests the auction semantics at the pinned release. A
registered proposal's text is public and, once its comment period closes, stable, but it is not
normative.

## Acceptance criterion stated by the enquiry

"An implementer MUST be able to build a SIGNET reverse-auction engine from the specification and
conformance fixtures alone, without the reference runtime, and obtain byte-equivalent close inputs
and the same semantic Award." Recorded as the criterion the remedy is measured against. Claim
triad for the deterministic close at v0.17.1: *modelled*; partly *tested*; not *certified*.

## Declined alternatives recorded at intake

- **Change core `Value.amount` to a string decimal.** Declined: breaking across every layer and
  EN 16931 alignment, to fix a problem the auction extension can fix locally.
- **Freeze a subset of the identity extension for the profile.** Declined: two definitions of one
  thing.
- **Treat the review as an internal Concert finding.** Declined: misattributes origin.

## What verification added

Two defects were found while checking the enquiry against the tree rather than raised by it:
the event hash is defined only in code (D-70), and chain scope differs between the adapter
contract and the auction runtime (D-71). Recorded here so the origin of each row is exact.
