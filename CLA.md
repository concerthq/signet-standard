> stated in [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md). The authoritative, legally

Thank you for contributing to SIGNET. To keep the standard open for everyone — and
to give every implementer the patent assurance a standard requires — Concert
Foundation asks each contributor to accept this Agreement before their contribution
is merged.

By signing (see *How to sign* below), **You** agree to the following with respect to
each **Contribution** You submit to this project.

## 1. Definitions

- **"You"** means the individual who submits a Contribution, or the legal entity on
  whose behalf the Contribution is submitted under a Corporate CLA.
- **"Contribution"** means any original work of authorship — code, schema, codelist
  values, documentation, examples, or other material — that You intentionally submit
  to this repository (e.g. via pull request), including any modification of an
  existing work.
- **"Concert"** means Concert Foundation, the steward of the SIGNET standard.

## 2. You keep ownership

This Agreement does **not** transfer ownership of Your Contribution. You retain all
right, title, and interest in it. You grant Concert the licences in §3 and §4 so the
standard can remain open; You remain free to use Your Contribution for any other
purpose.

Acceptance of Your Contribution under this Agreement gives no contributor — and no

If You are contributing on behalf of an organisation, an authorised signatory should
execute the **Corporate CLA** (one signature covers all of the organisation's staff)
rather than signing individually. Contact <hello@concert.foundation>.

## How to sign

This project uses an automated CLA assistant. When You open Your first pull request,
a bot will check whether You have signed. If You have not, it will comment with a
link to this document and ask You to add a single comment on the pull request:

> **I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA**

Your GitHub username and the pull-request reference are then recorded as Your
signature in the `signatures/` store on the `cla-signatures` branch. Signing once
covers all of Your future contributions to this repository under this CLA version. A
new **major** version of the CLA requires re-signing.

Questions: <hello@concert.foundation>.
